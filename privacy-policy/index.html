Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

🔒 Your JSON data never leaves your device. All processing happens entirely in your browser. We do not transmit, store, or have access to any JSON you paste or load into this tool.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to Format JSON, available at https://www.format-json.net/ ("the Site", "we", "us", or "our"). The Site provides free, browser-based JSON formatting, validation, and related developer tools.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: privacy@format-json.net

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We do not collect, store, or transmit any JSON data, code snippets, or other content you enter into the tool. All processing is performed locally in your web browser using JavaScript. No content you input is ever sent to our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically by our analytics provider and web infrastructure:

  • Pages visited and time spent on those pages
  • Your browser type, version, and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Referring website (how you arrived at the Site)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Screen resolution

This data is collected in aggregated, anonymised form. We do not use this data to identify individual users.

2.3 Local Storage & Session Storage

The Site may save lightweight preferences (such as your chosen colour theme: dark or light) to your browser's localStorage. This data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings.

3. Cookies

We use cookies solely for analytics purposes via Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager. No marketing, advertising, or tracking cookies from third parties other than Google are used.

Analytics Cookies

  • _ga — Google Analytics. Distinguishes users. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_* — Google Analytics 4. Persists session state. Expires after 2 years.
  • _gid — Google Analytics. Distinguishes users over 24 hours. Expires after 24 hours.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You can also refuse cookies by adjusting your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use any of the JSON tools on this Site.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (property ID: G-4SQS6ZLMB3) to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics collects usage data and processes it in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We have enabled IP anonymisation. We do not share any personally identifiable data with Google.

4.2 Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager (container ID: GTM-KBB4RLGW) to manage the deployment of analytics scripts. Google Tag Manager itself does not collect personal data; it only facilitates the loading of other tags. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

4.3 Content Delivery

The Site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers / Pages infrastructure. Cloudflare may log standard server access logs (IP addresses, request paths, timestamps) for security and performance purposes. These logs are handled in accordance with Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Your Information

The limited analytics data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Understand which features are most useful to our visitors
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Improve the overall performance and usability of the Site
  • Measure the reach of the Site (page views, unique visitors)

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using the information described above is our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding how our free tool is used and improving it. We process the minimum data necessary for this purpose.

Where cookies are placed that are not strictly necessary for the operation of the Site, we rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your browser's cookie settings or using the Google Analytics opt-out tool linked above.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

EEA / UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Right to access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restrict processing — Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — Withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

  • Right to know — Request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you.
  • Right to delete — Request deletion of personal information we have collected.
  • Right to opt out of sale — We do not sell personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@format-json.net. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

Analytics data collected via Google Analytics is retained for 26 months, after which it is automatically deleted. We do not maintain any other databases of user data. Because we do not receive your JSON content, there is no such data to retain or delete.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Links to Other Websites

The Site may contain links to external websites (for example, MDN, GitHub, or other developer resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do so. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
